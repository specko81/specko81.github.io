Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Jarvis is a private personal assistant used only by its owner for his own household. This policy explains how Jarvis handles Google user data.

Data accessed

When the owner signs in with his Google account, Jarvis may access that account's Gmail messages and basic profile information (name and email address), only within the permissions shown on the Google consent screen.

How the data is used

  • Solely to provide the assistant's features to the owner, such as reading, summarizing and organizing his own emails.
  • Data is processed on a private server in the owner's home.
  • Data is not sold, not shared with third parties, not used for advertising and not used to train general AI models.

Storage and security

Access tokens and any data needed for the assistant are stored only on the owner's private server and are not published anywhere.

Revoking access and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google Account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions). After revocation, Jarvis can no longer access the account, and locally stored data can be deleted by the owner.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Jarvis's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
