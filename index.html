Jarvis

A private, single-household personal assistant

Jarvis is a personal assistant application built and used by one private individual for his own household. It is not a commercial product and is not offered to the public.

What it does

  • Reads and organizes the owner's own Gmail messages (for example summarizing new emails and highlighting important ones).
  • Controls the owner's own smart home devices, such as lights and a robot vacuum.
  • Can be operated through a private chat bot, a wall-mounted tablet and a smartwatch.

Jarvis runs on a private server in the owner's home. Signing in with Google is only used so that the owner can connect his own Google account.
